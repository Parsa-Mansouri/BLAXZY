# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Film directors and creators who already know how to shoot and edit but find generative-AI tools
(model choice, prompting, reference handling) hard to learn. They work at a desk, usually in a
dim room, on a large screen, and judge software against the professional and Apple tools they
already use. A second audience, experienced AI users such as the owner, will be served later by
a separate Pro sector.

## Product Purpose

BLAXZY takes a user from an idea or a script to every generated clip of a film or series,
through four guided stages: Style, Script, Pre-production, Production. Success is a director
finishing a project without having to learn how the underlying AI tools work.

## Positioning

Other generators return one clip. BLAXZY returns the whole production, organised by episode,
scene and block, ready for an editor, and it makes the model and prompting decisions for the
user.

## Operating Context

- Self-hosted web application; desktop first, usable on tablet and phone.
- Long-running work (script writing, asset sheets, video generation) happens in the background
  while the user continues.
- Every output is reviewed by the user: accept, or reject with a reason, until it is right.
- Deliverable is individual clips; assembling them is done in the user's own editor.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Accounts, a home screen of projects, and the four-stage Wizard. Stages 1 and 2 are being built
  first; generation (Higgsfield) and credits come later.
- English only.
- Users never choose models in the Wizard.
- The terminology is fixed: project, episode, script, asset, sheet, block, stage names as above.
- Undecided: pricing and credit model, payment method, the Pro sector's design.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and wordmark: BLAXZY, set as wide-tracked capitals. The owner approved the wordmark as it
  stands.
- The interface is black. The owner approved black as the ground.
- The interface follows the owner-approved mockups (November 2026): calm and Apple-like, a
  floating rounded library sidebar, 48px capital page titles that rise in with a white rule,
  Geist type (Geist Mono for status lines, Courier Prime on paper for screenplay text), exactly
  three levels of hierarchy per screen, and one motion curve. Not Liquid Glass.
- No colour accent until the owner's brand identity arrives: white is the accent, and one accent
  token is reserved for the brand colour.
- Rejected by the owner as "AI-looking": blue or indigo accents, bordered rounded boxes around
  everything, a narrow centred column with oversized headings and empty space, the previous
  typeface, and chatty copy such as "Welcome back".
- Every control is custom-drawn; nothing may look like a chatbot.

## Product Principles

1. Nobody has to learn it: one clear next step on every screen, with help available in place.
2. The film is the subject: the user's titles, scripts and images lead; the interface recedes.
3. We decide the technical details; the user decides the creative ones.
4. Nothing is lost: every version and every decision is kept.
5. Tell the truth about progress, cost and failure in plain words.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full keyboard operation with a visible focus indicator, WCAG AA contrast on black, and respect
for reduced-motion settings.
